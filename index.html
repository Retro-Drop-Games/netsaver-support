A receipt didn't read right

Lay the receipt flat in good light and fill the frame. For long receipts, use Add › Scan a Long Receipt and take it in overlapping sections. You can fix anything on the review screen before saving, and tap an item's icon to move it to another budget. NetSaver remembers that next time.

Getting email receipts in

Open the receipt in Mail (or any email app), tap Share, then NetSaver. To bring receipts in automatically, connect an email account in Settings › Connections with an app password from your email provider.

Gift cards

Scan the front, then the back. NetSaver reads the store, value and last four digits, and blacks out any PIN. If a card was read wrong, open it and tap Rescan Card.

Syncing between devices

NetSaver syncs through your own iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on each device and that Settings › Security & Sync › iCloud Sync is on in NetSaver.

Store Alerts

Store Alerts let you know when you walk into a store where you have a gift card or a return you can still make. They need location access set to Always in the Settings app. Your location stays on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase All Data removes everything on your iPhone and in iCloud. Deleting the app removes what's stored on that device.

Privacy

Your receipts and spending stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. No ads, no tracking, no account. Read the full privacy policy.
