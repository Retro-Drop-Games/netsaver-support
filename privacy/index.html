NetSaver Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

The Short Version

  • Your receipts and spending stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.
  • There are no ads, no tracking and no account to create.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • When you look up an item's price, only its name or barcode is sent, never your photo or your spending.

What NetSaver Keeps

NetSaver keeps your receipts, items, budgets, categories, income, savings goals, card purchases, gift cards and the photos you take.

  • On your device. Everything is stored on your iPhone, inside NetSaver, its widget and its share extension.
  • In your iCloud. If iCloud Sync is on (Settings › Security & Sync), the same data is kept in your private iCloud database, so your other devices stay up to date. It's protected by your Apple Account, and we can't see it.
  • Read on your iPhone. Receipts, gift cards and photos are read on your device with Apple's text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence.
  • Diagnostics stay on your device. Developer diagnostics are never sent anywhere.

What Leaves Your Device

Only the following, and only when you use the feature:

  • Item prices. When you photograph an item, its name or barcode (and, for Kroger, the store you picked) goes to NetSaver's price server. The server asks eBay, Best Buy or Kroger for prices and keeps the answers for a few hours so it can share them between searches. It doesn't receive your photo and doesn't record who searched. Food names and barcodes may also be looked up in Open Food Facts and Open Prices.
  • Web search. "Find the Price on the Web" opens your chosen search engine inside NetSaver, just as if you searched in Safari.
  • Store Alerts. To find nearby stores where you have a gift card or an open return, the store's name and your approximate area are sent to Apple Maps. Your location itself isn't stored or sent to us.
  • Email accounts. If you connect an email account, NetSaver signs in to your email provider directly from your iPhone and reads messages there to find receipts. Your app password stays in your iPhone's Keychain. We never receive your email or password.

Features That Use Your Data

  • Sharing from Mail. Receipts you share to NetSaver are read on your iPhone.
  • Apple Wallet. If you allow it, NetSaver reads your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions with Apple's FinanceKit to log purchases and paychecks. You can turn this off anytime in the Settings app.
  • Apple Pay automation. If you set up the Shortcuts automation, each tap's store name and amount are added to NetSaver on your iPhone. Card details are never shared.
  • Location (Store Alerts). NetSaver uses your location on your iPhone to notice when you arrive at a store where you have a gift card or an open return. You can turn this off in Settings › Reminders.
  • Face ID. If you turn on the lock, Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. NetSaver never sees your face data.
  • Spotlight. Unless you turn it off, receipt and gift card summaries are added to your iPhone's own Spotlight search. They're removed while the Face ID lock is on.

What We Don't Do

  • No advertising or analytics tools.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No sign-up or account.

Your Choices

  • Delete your data. Settings › Erase All Data removes your receipts, card purchases, budgets, income, goals, gift cards and diagnostics, on this iPhone and in iCloud.
  • Disconnect email. Remove email accounts in Settings › Connections.
  • Turn off iCloud. Use Settings › Security & Sync to stop syncing.
  • Delete the app. This removes everything stored on your iPhone.

Children

NetSaver isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app (Settings › Privacy Policy) with a new date.
